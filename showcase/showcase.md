# Refract

Draw the light. On a quiet optical bench, three shapes become three beams: cool mint triangles, amber squares, and violet diamonds. Each begins and ends at an outlined emitter. The filled lenses between them are yours to thread.

Press, trace, release. Your beam stays where you leave it, and each new connection changes the picture. Travel diagonally, weave through tight corners, and cross neutral crystals to spend their charges. Every lens must be threaded, every charge spent exactly, and no two segments may cross.

A wrong turn is a chance to think again. Backtrack to unwind a beam, pick up either end to continue, or clear the bench and see a different route. There is no ticking clock. The satisfying moment arrives when the last connection brings every channel into balance.

Campaign takes you through 24 carefully arranged studies, from a single beam to a dense interplay of three channels and shared crystals. Cascade offers an endless sequence of fresh boards, rising through five tiers as you solve. Find your rhythm, follow a possibility, and watch the whole bench light up.
